- **D1/D2:** `DISTINCT ON(audience) … ORDER BY audience, revision DESC` has no state filter. The outer query locks with `FOR UPDATE OF n` in audience order. A notice is forwarded only if it is `sending`, or `queued`/`failed` with `attempts<5`, and its revision equals the old case revision.
